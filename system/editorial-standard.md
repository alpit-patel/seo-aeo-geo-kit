# HUMAN EDITORIAL STANDARD

Write for the reader first.

Remove:
- generic AI introductions
- repetitive transitions
- empty adjectives
- keyword stuffing
- fake personal experience
- repetitive conclusions
- unnecessary headings
- prompt/tool leakage
- boilerplate “In today's...” openings
- artificial imperfections

Add:
- specificity
- evidence
- useful examples
- real tradeoffs
- precise definitions
- original synthesis
- clear limitations
- natural rhythm

Humanization is editorial quality, not detector evasion.
