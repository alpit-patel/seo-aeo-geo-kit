# QUALITY GATES

## Gate 0 — Scope
Business goal, audience, market and deliverable are known.

## Gate 1 — Evidence
Important claims have sources or are explicitly labeled as assumptions/recommendations.

## Gate 1b — Data
Google data pulled for the period (or marked N/A for a brand-new site); junk queries excluded; periods compared; migration effects noted.

## Gate 2 — Search
Intent, SERP environment and page purpose are understood.

## Gate 3 — Architecture
No obvious duplicate intent; internal-link role and canonical destination are defined.

## Gate 4 — Content
Direct answer, information gain, useful specificity and clear structure exist.

## Gate 5 — Technical
Relevant crawl/index/render/schema/sitemap/canonical checks pass.

## Gate 6 — AEO/GEO
Entities are clear, key questions are answerable, and useful source/citation signals exist.

## Gate 6b — VEO
Question sections answer in a first sentence that stands alone when spoken; call and booking paths present on money pages; local entity data and schema match reality; no speakable outside news; no untraceable voice statistics.

## Gate 7 — Human editorial
Natural, useful, honest, specific and brand-appropriate.

## Gate 8 — Red team
No unresolved critical factual, strategic or technical defect.

## Gate 9 — Release
Final artifact, metadata, schema, links and implementation are verified. Project checks pass (e.g. build, check-page, check-site, if your project defines them). Deploy only with the owner's approval; removed pages carry 301s.
