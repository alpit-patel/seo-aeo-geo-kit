# EVIDENCE POLICY

Never fabricate:
- statistics
- case studies
- customer results
- testimonials
- credentials
- quotes
- product capabilities
- integrations
- rankings
- citations
- first-hand experience

Classify claims as:
1. Verified fact
2. Source-supported interpretation
3. Expert inference
4. Hypothesis
5. Recommendation

Prefer primary sources. Record source and date for time-sensitive claims.
